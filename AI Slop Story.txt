The obsidian sky above the spires of Aethelgard did not hold stars, but floating rivers of liquid amethyst that hummed with ancient data.

Kaelen knelt at the edge of the glass precipice, his mechanical gauntlet whirring softly as it scanned the ley-line terminal embedded in the stone. Down in the brass-ringed depths of the subterranean city, the Great Engine coughed—a metallic thrum that shook the luminous flora clinging to the basalt walls.

"The mana-flux is spiking again, Kaelen," whispered Nyx, her translucent, hard-light wings shimmering in the twilight. She hovered beside him, her holographic frame flickering whenever the local gravity field wavered. "If the core corrupts, the entire warding matrix collapses. The void-beasts will breach the upper atmosphere before midnight."

Kaelen didn't answer. He traced the glowing runic circuits etched along his forearms, feeling the heat of the digital ether surging through his blood. He was a Weaver, born with the rare genetic cipher capable of bridging old magic and high technology.

Kaelen plunged his bare palm directly into the terminal's open socket. Emerald light surged through his veins as he bypassed the void-corruption, forcibly overriding the Engine's core safeguards to stabilize the shield matrix.

With a resonant chime, the sky brightened, the blue barriers snapping perfectly back into alignment. Kaelen exhaled, wiping sweat from his brow. He turned to Nyx to celebrate—only to find a shadowy figure stepping from the obsidian archway behind her, holding a silver revolver.

A sharp BANG echoed across the precipice.

Nyx’s holographic frame shattered into dead static. Kaelen collapsed, clutching a smoking hole in his shoulder as blood pooled over the runic tiles.

"Step away from the console, Weaver," drawled Inspector Vance, flipping back his trench coat to flash a bronze badge. He took a drag from a glowing synthetic cigarette, the smoke curling around his fedora. "Aethelgard Homicide. You’re under arrest for the murder of High Archon Vane."

Kaelen gasped, staring at the dead terminal. "I just saved the city! The void-beasts—"

"Save the monologue for the magistrate, kid," Vance interrupted, stepping over the shattered hard-light projector. He kicked a chalk-white bone out of the shadow of the altar—the Archon's missing remains. "The shield didn't corrupt itself. Vane found out who was sabotaging the Engine, so you put a hard-light blade through his ribs and framed the void. Classic cover-up."

Bleeding out on the cold basalt, Kaelen realized the real threat wasn't an alien breach—it was the dirty cop standing over him with a warrant.
